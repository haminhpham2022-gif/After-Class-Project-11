An introduction to Fruits

Strawberry

The strawberry is a widely grown plant cultivated worldwide for its fruit. The fruit is appreciated for its aroma, bright red colour, juicy texture, and sweetness. It is eaten either fresh or in prepared foods such as jam, ice cream, and chocolates.

Peach

The peach is a deciduous tree that bears edible juicy fruits with various characteristics. The tree is regarded as handsome and is planted in gardens for its springtime blooms in addition to fruit production.

Orange

The orange belongs to the citrus fruits, which are among the oldest fruits in the world. It originates from the region between northeastern India and southwestern China.
